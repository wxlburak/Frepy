# Frepy
Frepy is a device hub for techtok.

Download the latest version from the Release section below. You don't need to delete the old version; you can update it.

Frequently Asked Questions 🤔 (QQA)

Question: How can I add my own brand?
Reply: [Step 1.] Open Frepy Login/Sign After Scroll all the way down from the profile section; you will see the application section. [Step 2.] Enter your brand name, then open Chrome, type "Pngup" in the search bar, go to the site, upload your photo, and then get a direct link and exit. [Step 3.] Go to Frepy, enter your brand name, and then place the link in the `brand URL` field. [Step 4.(Final)] Submit your application and wait for approval. Once approved, a tab will open for your brand where you can add and edit devices.
